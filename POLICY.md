# YipLoader Policies

## Code of Conduct

1. Do not discriminate against or harass others. Bigotory, including racism, homophobia, transphobia, and any other kind of harassment is strictly not allowed.
2. Do not spam in any form. Some off-topic discussion is okay, but try to keep it to dedicated off-topic sections.
3. Try to avoid drama and keep conversations productive. Remember to discuss the idea and not the person who had it.
4. Remember to protect your own privacy and censor or filter logs and bug reports as apporpriate.
5. Please keep all communication in somewhat comprehensible English. Use of translation tools is allowed such that you disclose their use.

## ABI and API Breakage Policy

YipLoader's policy is that **the ABI shall never break**, except in extreme circumstances and where the breakage is unlikely to affect most projects.

YipLoader itself is a relatively simple project, and should not often need to break ABI so long as it is designed well in the first place.

If a new version of a structure or method needs to be created, it shall append a "v2", "v3", "v4" or etc. to the name. For example, a new version of `YipModInfo` should be named `YipModInfov2`.

## LLM/"AI" Policy

**"AI"-generated content is not allowed** within the YipLoader community as a whole. This includes, but is not limited to, any code created with the assistance of "AI" (even if the code itself was written by a human) and issues, code reviews, and comments made with the assistance of "AI".

In addition to the usual concerns, please consider that use of "AI" is an immense violation of your own free will, and the free will of others: it strips you of your own ability to make decisions and think critically, and it forces others to interact with what is essentially nothing more than noise.
